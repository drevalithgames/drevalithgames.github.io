Upcoming Windows PC Horror Escape Game

VOLT & VITALS

Solo Mode  •  Two-Player Multiplayer Co-op

Volt & Vitals is an upcoming Windows PC horror escape game set inside a decaying medical house filled with locked rooms, unstable electrical systems, surveillance cameras, and a dangerous Doctor.

Experience the nightmare alone in Solo Mode or team up with a friend in Two-Player Multiplayer Co-op—explore the house freely, search rooms, locate essential items, solve mechanical puzzles, distract the Doctor, and work together to escape.
